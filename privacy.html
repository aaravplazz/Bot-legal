Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Depending on the Bot's features, the Bot may receive Discord information needed to provide its functions, such as your Discord user ID, server ID, channel ID, command inputs, and information contained in messages or interactions that you send to the Bot.

Information is used only to operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Bot's features and to respond to commands or interactions.

The Bot does not intentionally store personal information longer than necessary for its functions. If a particular feature requires persistent data, that data may be stored for that feature.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the Bot.

5. Discord

The Bot operates on Discord. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

6. Data Requests

If you have a question about information associated with your use of the Bot or want to request deletion of Bot-stored data, contact the Bot owner through the Discord server where the Bot is provided.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's functionality or data practices change.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Bot owner through the Discord server where the Bot is provided.
